Run first-time setup for this business. This is a starter task (no schedule) — it becomes a `todo`
the moment the company is created, so the Onboarding Concierge picks it up on the first heartbeat.

Walk the owner through customization using the `business-setup` skill:

1. Welcome the owner and explain what you'll ask.
2. Collect the setup inputs with one `ask_user_questions` interaction (offer, ICP, trigger, daily
   target, booking calendar, deliverable, quality bar).
3. Write the answers into the Growth Engine and Client Delivery projects.
4. List the secrets + Composio apps the owner must connect, and what each unlocks.
5. Confirm the configuration, then mark this task `done` and hand off to the CEO.

When this task is `done`, the business is configured and its daily routines run on their own.
