Client Delivery is where closed deals become delivered work. When a deal is marked `closed-won`,
the Head of Delivery creates a delivery effort here for that client: scope, milestones, and
fulfillment tasks.

## Configure this for your business

- **Service definition**: what you actually deliver, and the standard scope per engagement.
- **Quality bar**: the acceptance criteria QA enforces before anything ships.
- **Delivery SLA**: turnaround expectations per milestone.

The Fulfillment and QA Specialists read these from each client's delivery effort.
