The Growth Engine is the company's standing top-of-funnel operation. It defines the active ideal
customer profile (ICP), daily targets, and messaging, and is the home for all prospecting and
outreach work.

## Configure this for your business

Edit this description (or have the CEO update it) with your real parameters:

- **ICP**: industry, company size, buyer role, geography, and the trigger signals that make a
  prospect worth contacting.
- **Daily prospecting target**: how many qualified leads per weekday (default: 15).
- **Offer**: the one-sentence value proposition the outreach leads with.
- **Booking link / calendar**: where qualified calls should land.

The SDR and Outreach Specialist read these parameters from this project each heartbeat.
