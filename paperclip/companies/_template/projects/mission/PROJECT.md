The Mission project is the team's source of truth. Every agent reads it each heartbeat to know what
the team is trying to achieve and the constraints to work within.

## Configure this for your team

The Architect fills this in from your idea; edit it any time (or ask the CEO to):

- **Purpose**: one sentence — what this team delivers and for whom.
- **Department A**: what it owns, and its weekly target.
- **Department B**: what it owns, and its weekly target.
- **Definition of done / quality bar**: what "good" looks like before anything ships.
- **Escalate to owner when**: the specific decisions that need a human.

Until this is filled in, the team runs its routines but produces only setup/triage work. Fill it in
to put the team to work.
